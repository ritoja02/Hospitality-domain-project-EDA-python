# 🏨 AtliQ Grands Business Analysis

## 📋 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the business performance of **AtliQ Grands**, a reputed hotel chain, by performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and data transformation on its booking datasets. The goal is to uncover patterns in revenue realized per city, identify key revenue drivers, compare occupancy rates and support strategic decision-making for business growth and optimization.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- ✅ Perform comprehensive EDA to understand the structure, quality, and characteristics of the dataset.
- ✅ Clean and transform raw data into an analysis-ready format by handling missing values and converting date fields.
- ✅ Generate time-based features (e.g., month, weekday) to analyze booking and revenue trends.
- ✅ Identify seasonal patterns, peak booking periods, and underutilized timeframes.
- ✅ Evaluate property-wise and room-category-wise performance to spot high-performing assets and improvement areas.
- ✅ Generate city-wise monthly revenue trends to decide the marketing spend in each.
